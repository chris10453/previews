Los auténticos tacos al pastor de trompo

That line is theirs, off the sign. It runs under the wordmark on the banner over the door, next to a painted trompo. Marcos Rojas ordered the al pastor volcán and wrote that al pastor is their specialty.
205 of the 220 reviews are five stars. That is Google's own breakdown, not a rounding. Kristina Fraser's line was that it is family owned and that she and her husband wanted to keep it to themselves.
The salsa gets its own mention. Izzy Cobos wrote that it was very flavorful with a nice kick, unlike the usual watered down salsas, and that the staff recommended the right tacos.

The card, as printed

Read off their own menu, line by line. The rest of the card runs burritos from $11.50, tortas from $10, and pastor by the pound at $31.
