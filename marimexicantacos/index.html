Off the chalk board

Every figure here was read off the board behind their counter, which you can see for yourself further down. Nothing rounded, nothing added.

Tacos, 3.25 to 3.95

Their board sets the terms: American style is lettuce, tomato and cheese, Mexican style is onions and cilantro. Veggies 3.25; tinga chicken, grill chicken, carnitas, ground beef and chorizo 3.50; steak or steak and chorizo 3.75; shrimp 3.95.

Three queso birria tacos, 14.50

Chalked with the consome included. Nixon ordered the birria with cheese, and Lauren Farrar's boyfriend called them fantastic.

Weekday lunch, 11.95

Monday to Friday, 11 to 2. Pick tacos, a burrito, empanadas, a quesadilla or sopes; the board says all five come with rice and beans.

Guacamole, 5.99

Eight ounces, or 8.49 with chips. Lauren Farrar calls it the freshest guac she has had at a restaurant. Cheese dip is 5.49, salsa with chips 7.50.
